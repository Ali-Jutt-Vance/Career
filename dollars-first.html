First Fascia

Italy means-tests its student grants on family income and property. You have a modest income and no property at all — which places you not merely inside the threshold but at the very bottom of the scale, where the awards are largest.

01

What this means in practice

Tuition goes to zero — you would owe only a regional tax of roughly €120–180 and a stamp duty. The DSU award lands in the top bracket rather than a reduced one. And because the asset side of the formula is genuinely empty rather than merely small, there is no borderline judgement for an assessor to make against you.

Two honest qualifications. This is an estimate — the exact figure depends on your household size, whether your family rents (rent is deductible, which would push the number lower still), and the euro rate on the day. And the ISEE must be truthful, not minimised: declaring incorrect figures obliges you to repay every euro received. You do not need to shade anything here. The real numbers are already at the bottom of the scale.

One more thing worth knowing: you will be assessed as part of your father's household, not as an independent applicant. Italy treats a student as independent only after two years living away from the family home and earning around €9,000 a year — thresholds you do not meet. That is fine. Being in the household is what makes your father's absent property work in your favour.

02

The problem the ISEE does not solve

A near-zero ISEE gets you free tuition and a large grant. It does not get you a visa, and this is where the Italian route actually bites.

The visa needs money the DSU has not paid yet

For 2026/27, Italy's proof-of-funds requirement rose 46% — from €6,947.33 to €10,179.85, roughly ₨ 3.26 million. The visa is decided months before your DSU result, and the consulate assesses not only whether the money exists but its provenance and traceability.

There is also a coherence problem people walk straight into. You will be telling one Italian authority that your household lives on €6,000 a year, and another that you have €10,179 available. Both can be true — but if the second figure is a lump sum that appeared in a relative's account last month, the file reads badly and refusals follow.

The fix: win a second, different scholarship

Italian universities award their own merit scholarships at the point of admission. That letter arrives months before your visa appointment, and it explains your funding in a way that is entirely consistent with a low ISEE. You then apply for the DSU separately once you have enrolled.

Two instruments, two purposes: the university scholarship gets you the visa, the DSU pays for your life. Applying for only one of them is the most common way this route fails.

05

The ISEE dossier, item by item

This is the document the whole route hangs on. It is assembled from Pakistani paperwork, legalised in Pakistan, and issued in Italy — three jurisdictions, which is why it takes months rather than weeks.

What you need, all dated 31 December 2025

  • Family composition certificate — every member of the household, from NADRA.
  • Income evidence for each earning member — your salary certificate and your father's, plus FBR tax returns if filed. Two earners, two sets.
  • Every bank and savings account balance as at 31 December 2025 — including accounts with nil or near-nil balances. Omitting an account is what gets files rejected, not having a small balance in one.
  • A property and land declaration — in your case a statement that the household owns no immovable property. This is a positive document to obtain, not an absence to explain: get it stated explicitly rather than leaving the question blank.
  • Vehicle registration for the motorcycle. Plates are collected as supporting documentation even though vehicles do not enter the ISEE calculation — declare it and let it sit at zero.
  • Rent agreement, if your family rents. Rent is deductible and would lower the figure further.

Then, in order

  1. MOFA apostille on each document. Pakistan is a Hague Convention member, so this replaces embassy legalisation — but note that digitally QR-verified copies are explicitly not accepted. You need the physical apostille.
  2. Sworn translation into Italian.
  3. Legalisation through the Italian consulate in Islamabad or Karachi.
  4. Submit to a CAF, which issues the ISEE Parificato in 10 to 20 days. Some accept scanned PDFs by email; others require originals in person in Italy. Confirm with the specific CAF before assuming you can do it remotely — this single question can decide whether you make the DSU deadline.
Start collecting in September, not June

Bank statements for a closed calendar year get progressively harder to obtain as time passes, and Pakistani branches are slow with historical records. Request the 31 December 2025 balances now, while they are a routine request rather than an archival one. Everything else can follow later.

08

Three ways this goes wrong

The credit requirement, from year two

Year one is assessed on income alone. After that you must have earned roughly 25 CFU by 10 August — and missing it means repaying the entire year's grant, as a legal obligation, not merely losing next year's. Clearing it between 10 August and 30 November gets you 50%.

This bears directly on your plans. Italy's 20-hours-a-week work right is real and you will want to use it, but hours billed to clients are hours not spent on exams. Treat the first year's coursework as the thing protecting your funding. Keep the agency to one or two retainers until year two is secured, then scale.

The permit that lapses

After graduating, Italy grants a twelve-month permesso di soggiorno per attesa occupazione — to find work or to start a business, which is the clause that matters for you. But you must apply before your study permit expires, having first registered with the employment office and obtained a DID certificate. A permit already lapsed cannot be converted: you would return to Pakistan and begin the visa again. Diarise it three months out and treat that date as immovable.

Reapplying every single year

The DSU is not renewed automatically. It is an annual application with a fresh ISEE each time, and a fresh deadline in September or October. Missing the second-year deadline is a common and entirely self-inflicted way to lose the grant.
