## Chapter Overview

This is a personal planning document, kept inside the book because it shares two critical dependencies with the *former* 137-day plan and must not drift out of sync with them:

Italy means-tests its student grants on family income and property. A modest income and no property at all places you not merely inside the threshold but at the very bottom of the scale — the *prima fascia* — where the awards are largest.

**Target intake: September 2027.** That is nine months after the career plan's deadline, which means the two are sequential rather than competing: close the job and the client income in 2026, use both to fund and evidence the visa in 2027.

## 1. What the numbers actually produce

Italy calculates need through the **ISEE**, a single figure combining household income and household assets. A father owning a motorcycle and nothing else matters a great deal, because of exactly how the formula treats assets.

```
ISE  = R + [(PM + PI) × 0.20]
ISEE = ISE ÷ p
```

**Estimated ISEE Parificato — reference year 2025**

| Component | Detail | Value |
|---|---|---|
| **R** — household income | ₨91,000/month plus father's ₨70,000/month, annualised | €6,037 |
| **PM** — movable assets | Bank accounts, deposits, shares, bonds. Nil savings, and a €10,000 allowance applies for a household of three or more regardless | €0 |
| **PI** — immovable assets | Land and buildings only, at cadastral value. **Vehicles are not immovable property** — the motorcycle does not enter the formula | €0 |
| **p** — equivalence scale | Household of four. Rises with each additional member, lowering the final figure further | ÷ 2.46 |
| **Estimated ISEE** | Roughly €2,100–3,000 depending on household size | **≈ €2,450** |

**Against the thresholds it must clear:**

| Threshold | Amount | Position |
|---|---|---|
| Your estimated position | €2,450 | Bottom of the scale — *prima fascia*, where awards are largest |
| DSU full package | €13,560 | Cash grant, dorm place, daily meals. You sit at roughly a fifth of it |
| Zero tuition | €24,500 | Complete fee exemption at public universities. Not close to a concern |

**In practice:** tuition goes to zero — you would owe only a regional tax of roughly €120–180 plus stamp duty. The DSU award lands in the top bracket rather than a reduced one. And because the asset side of the formula is genuinely empty rather than merely small, there is no borderline judgement for an assessor to make against you.

One more thing worth knowing: you will be assessed as part of your father's household, not as an independent applicant. Italy treats a student as independent only after two years living away from the family home and earning around €9,000 a year — thresholds you do not meet. That is fine. Being in the household is what makes the absent property work in your favour.

## 2. The problem the ISEE does not solve

A near-zero ISEE gets you free tuition and a large grant. **It does not get you a visa**, and this is where the Italian route actually bites.

### The visa needs money the DSU has not paid yet

For 2026/27, Italy's proof-of-funds requirement rose 46% — from €6,947.33 to **€10,179.85**, roughly **₨3.26 million**. The visa is decided months before your DSU result, and the consulate assesses not only whether the money exists but its **provenance and traceability**.

There is also a coherence problem people walk straight into. You will be telling one Italian authority that your household lives on €6,000 a year, and another that you have €10,179 available. Both can be true — but if the second figure is a lump sum that appeared in a relative's account last month, the file reads badly and refusals follow.

### The fix: win a second, different scholarship

Italian universities award their own **merit scholarships at the point of admission**. That letter arrives months before your visa appointment, and it explains your funding in a way that is entirely consistent with a low ISEE. You then apply for the DSU separately once you have enrolled.

**Two instruments, two purposes:** the university scholarship gets you the visa; the DSU pays for your life. Applying for only one of them is the most common way this route fails.

## 5. The ISEE dossier, item by item

This is the document the whole route hangs on. It is assembled from Pakistani paperwork, legalised in Pakistan, and issued in Italy — three jurisdictions, which is why it takes months rather than weeks.

### What you need, all dated 31 December 2025

1. **Family composition certificate** — every member of the household, from NADRA.
2. **Income evidence for each earning member** — your salary certificate and your father's, plus FBR tax returns if filed. Two earners, two sets.
3. **Every bank and savings account balance as at 31 December 2025** — *including accounts with nil or near-nil balances*. Omitting an account is what gets files rejected, not having a small balance in one.
4. **A property and land declaration** — a statement that the household owns no immovable property. This is a positive document to obtain, not an absence to explain: get it stated explicitly rather than leaving the question blank.
5. **Vehicle registration** for the motorcycle. Plates are collected as supporting documentation even though vehicles do not enter the ISEE calculation — declare it and let it sit at zero.
6. **Rent agreement**, if the family rents. Rent is deductible and would lower the figure further.

### Then, in order

1. **MOFA apostille** on each document. Pakistan is a Hague Convention member, so this replaces embassy legalisation — but **digitally QR-verified copies are explicitly not accepted**. You need the physical apostille.
2. **Sworn translation into Italian.**
3. **Legalisation** through the Italian consulate in Islamabad or Karachi.
4. **Submit to a CAF**, which issues the ISEE Parificato in 10–20 days. Some accept scanned PDFs by email; others require originals in person in Italy. **Confirm with the specific CAF before assuming you can do it remotely** — this single question can decide whether you make the DSU deadline.

### Start collecting in September, not June

Bank statements for a closed calendar year get progressively harder to obtain as time passes, and Pakistani branches are slow with historical records. Request the 31 December 2025 balances now, while it is a routine request rather than an archival one. Everything else can follow later.

## 6. Documents already handled

The IBCC verification of the matric and inter sanads is the correct first link and cannot be substituted — **HEC does not attest school-level certificates at all**. The subject-wise marks on them matter more than most people realise: Italian credit-equivalence assessment maps schooling subject by subject, and aggregate-only certificates stall there.

| Link | Covers | Status |
|---|---|---|
| IBCC | Matric and inter sanads | Done |
| HEC e-attestation | BS IT degree and transcript. ₨3,000 each, online since 11 May 2026, 8–10 days | **Next** |
| MOFA apostille | Degree, transcript, sanads, and the whole ISEE dossier | After HEC |
| CIMEA or *Dichiarazione di Valore* | Italy's recognition of the degree. CIMEA is online, ~€300, ~15 working days | Before applying |
| Embassy attestation | The old per-country step | **Not needed** |

Two things have collapsed the expected work. HEC moved to a fully online, paperless system on 11 May 2026 — no office visit, no originals, a blockchain e-Attestation certificate in 8–10 days. And Pakistan's accession to the Hague Apostille Convention means MOFA issues an apostille that Italy accepts directly, with no consular legalisation of academic documents on top.

**Note the asymmetry:** academic documents need apostille only, but the ISEE **financial** documents still require consular legalisation *and* sworn translation. One process does not cover both.

### Where the gap money comes from

You have a full year before most of this is needed. **Freelance work billed in dollars — even at a modest $700 a month, which is well within reach of the work Phase 8 and Phase 11 already cover — closes the entire ₨947,000 inside twelve months without touching your salary.** It also builds a documented, tax-filed income trail, which strengthens the visa file rather than complicating it.

## 8. Three ways this goes wrong

**The credit requirement, from year two.** Year one is assessed on income alone. From year two the grant is conditional on earning roughly 25 CFU by 10 August, and failure is a *repayment obligation* rather than simply a loss of the grant. Keep freelance work to one or two retainers until that bar is cleared.

**The permit that lapses.** The residence permit must be applied for within **eight working days** of arrival, and when you graduate you must register with the employment office for a DID certificate and apply for the twelve-month *attesa occupazione* permit **before your study permit expires**. An expired permit cannot be converted; it sends you home to start again.

**Reapplying every single year.** The grant never renews automatically — new ISEE, new application, every year, to the regional agency.

## 9. Step by step, start to finish

Thirty steps across seven stages. Steps 1–7 are all available this month, and four of them cost nothing.

**Stage A — Now (Aug–Sep 2026)**
1. Book IELTS Academic. *(₨78,000 — shared with the 137-day plan, exam 14 Nov 2026)*
2. Renew passport if within 18 months of expiry. *(₨15,000)*
3. Request all household bank balances as at 31 December 2025. *(free)*
4. Obtain the NADRA family composition certificate. *(free/nominal)*
5. Obtain salary certificates for both earners. *(free)*
6. Obtain the no-immovable-property declaration explicitly. *(free)*
7. Begin HEC e-attestation for degree and transcript. *(₨6,000, 8–10 days)*

**Stage B — Build (Sep–Dec 2026)**
8. Ship two public AI automation projects. *(This is Projects 2 and 3 of the 137-day plan — no extra work.)*
9. Start dollar-billed freelance income. *(Phase 11 — the funding mechanism.)*
10. Collect and file all calendar-2025 financial records.
11. Sit IELTS, 14 Nov 2026. Target Band 7.5+.
12. Order the CIMEA statement of comparability. *(~€300, ~15 working days)*

**Stage C — Admissions (Jan–May 2027)**
13. Shortlist five universities; confirm each one's scholarship-at-admission mechanism.
14. Write the motivation letter; adapt per university.
15. Secure two academic or professional references.
16. Submit all five applications **with the scholarship form attached to each**.
17. Add three or four Erasmus Mundus consortia with Italian partners.
18. Accept an offer; obtain the scholarship award letter in writing.

**Stage D — Dossier (Jun–Jul 2027)**
19. **Universitaly pre-enrolment by 31 July.** *Hard gate.*
20. MOFA apostille on every item from stages A and C. *(~₨12,000)*
21. Sworn Italian translations, then consular legalisation at Islamabad or Karachi. *(~₨45,000)* Book the consular appointment as soon as translations are underway.
22. Submit the dossier to a CAF for the ISEE Parificato. *(~₨20,000, 10–20 days)* **Ask first: will this CAF issue remotely from scanned PDFs, or does it require originals in person in Italy?** Contact three CAFs and use whichever confirms remote issuance in writing.

**Stage E — Visa (Aug–Nov 2027)**
23. Book the visa appointment the day pre-enrolment clears. Book first, assemble the file second.
24. **Submit the visa application by 30 November.** *Hard gate.* Lead with the university scholarship letter, then whatever funds you hold, against €10,179.85. Bring the accommodation booking, health insurance, CIMEA statement, and Universitaly confirmation. Expect questions about source of funds and answer them with documents, not explanations.

**Stage F — Arrival (Sep–Oct 2027)**
25. Fly, enrol, obtain the *codice fiscale* in your first week — it is needed for a bank account, a phone contract, and the residence permit.
26. **Apply for the *permesso di soggiorno* within 8 working days.** *Hard gate.* Kit from a post office *sportello amici*, then the Questura.
27. **File the DSU application, 5 Sep – 31 Oct.** *Hard gate.* To your university's regional agency — ER.GO, ESU, EDiSU, or DiSCo Lazio. Tuscany closes first. This deadline can fall before your visa is issued, so check your region's date the moment you accept an offer and work backwards.
28. Apply **separately** for dormitory accommodation and the canteen card. Housing is usually a distinct application with its own deadline, even though both flow from the same ISEE. Missing it means paying private rent while holding a free dorm entitlement.

**Stage G — Holding it (Dec 2027 onward)**
29. Survive to the first payment (3–5 months), then protect the credits. Keep freelance work to one or two retainers until the 25 CFU bar is cleared. *(~₨450,000 gap to bridge)*
30. Reapply for the DSU annually, and convert the permit before it expires.
