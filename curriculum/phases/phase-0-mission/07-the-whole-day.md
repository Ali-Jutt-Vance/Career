## Chapter Overview

Since 7 October 2026 the plan in this book runs at **thirty hours a week** — the fifty-week plan repacked into about half the time, closing on 27 March 2027. Your life is one hundred and sixty-eight hours. This chapter is about all of them, because the other one hundred and thirty-eight decide whether the thirty actually happen.

You are 24. The goals are clear, and they are bigger than a job:

- **Career:** a real backend engineering job at 3–5× today's salary — this book.

- **Body:** strong, fit and healthy — gym five days a week, the park every weekend morning.

- **Marriage at 28:** arriving there healthy, stable, and ready.

These are not eight separate projects. They are one project, and the daily timetable is where it lives. Every goal above is either *in* the timetable, or it is a wish.

## The gym — five days, one hour

Five days, one hour, 20:00–21:00. The goal is strength, a lean waist and good posture, not a bodybuilding show. Train hard, finish by 21:00, and **never use a stimulant pre-workout** — caffeine at 20:00 is still in your blood at 01:00 and will wreck the sleep everything else depends on.

| Day | Session | Main lifts |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Upper — push focus | Bench press, overhead press, incline dumbbell press, lateral raises, triceps |
| **Tue** | Lower | Squat, Romanian deadlift, leg press, calves, plank |
| **Wed** | Upper — pull focus | Pull-ups or lat pulldown, barbell or cable row, face pulls, rear delts, biceps |
| **Thu** | Lower + core | Deadlift (light-moderate), Bulgarian split squat, leg curl, hanging knee raise |
| **Fri** | Full body + conditioning | 3 compound lifts at moderate weight, then 15 min bike or rowing intervals |

**The rules:**

1. **Warm up for 8 minutes** every time. An injury costs you months.
2. **Progressive overload.** Write every set in a notebook or app. Add a little weight or one rep when the last session was clean.
3. **3–4 working sets, 6–12 reps**, the last one or two reps hard but with good form.
4. **Learn the form before the weight.** Ask a trainer to check your squat, deadlift and bench in the first two weeks.
5. **Absolutely no steroids or "cycles".** Anabolic steroids shut down your own testosterone and can stop sperm production entirely — the exact opposite of what you are trying to fix. This is not negotiable.
6. **Deload every 6–8 weeks** — one lighter week. Progress continues because of it, not in spite of it.

## Health — the doctors first

You named three concerns: **hair loss, sperm quality and erectile problems.** At 24 all three are common, most have clear causes, and most respond to proper treatment — *when they are diagnosed properly*. The worst thing you can do is guess, and the second worst is to buy something from an advert.

> **This section is general information, not medical advice.** It exists to get you to the right doctor with the right questions, early.

### Do not self-medicate

- **Avoid "mardana taqat" tonics, desi hakeem powders and online "timing" pills.** Some of these products have been found to contain hidden prescription drugs or steroids, in unknown doses. They can hide the real cause and harm you.
- **Do not take ED tablets or hair-loss tablets without a doctor.** Both can be correct treatments — prescribed, at the right dose, after the right tests.

### The first-30-days health checklist

| # | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Book a **urologist / andrologist** appointment. Tell them everything, including sleep, stress and screen habits. They have heard it all before. | Urologist |
| 2 | Ask about a **semen analysis** (usually after 2–7 days of abstinence) | Urologist |
| 3 | Ask about blood tests: **fasting glucose / HbA1c, lipid profile, morning testosterone, prolactin, TSH (thyroid), vitamin D, ferritin, CBC** | Your doctor |
| 4 | Book a **dermatologist** for hair loss — ask for a diagnosis first (pattern hair loss, deficiency, thyroid, stress shedding and others look similar) | Dermatologist |
| 5 | Check **blood pressure and weight / waist** — write both down in BASELINE.md | Any clinic or pharmacy |
| 6 | If anxiety, low mood or performance anxiety is part of it, see a **psychologist**. That is treatment, not weakness. | Psychologist |

### What you control, starting today

| Concern | Lifestyle factors with good evidence |
|---|---|
| **Sperm quality** | No smoking, vaping, sheesha or naswar. Healthy weight. Sleep. **Keep the testes cool** — no laptop on your lap, no very hot baths, loose cotton underwear. No steroids. Sperm take roughly **three months** to develop, so improvements show in the test about three months later — which is why you start now. |
| **Erections** | Blood flow and nerves: regular exercise, healthy weight, normal blood sugar and blood pressure, no smoking, enough sleep, lower stress. Many young men also find that **cutting pornography** helps — it belongs with the digital rules below. Anxiety plays a large part at your age; the doctor and, if needed, a psychologist can address it. |
| **Hair** | Early diagnosis matters — **treatment protects the hair you still have much better than it regrows what is gone.** Eat enough protein, iron and vitamin D; sleep; manage stress. Medicines that work for pattern hair loss exist, and some can affect sexual function in a minority of men — which is exactly why the dermatologist and the urologist should both know about each other's findings. |

**Retest in three months.** Put the repeat semen analysis and blood tests in your calendar for **January 2027**. Improvement you can measure is the best motivation there is.

## The digital rules — getting your attention back

A whole season in a day is not rest. It is the same loop that took three years of your coding ability: easy dopamine, now, instead of hard reward, later. Self-control is not a feeling you wait for; it is **an environment you build** so that the easy choice is the right one.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **No series or anime Monday–Friday** | None. The evenings are gym, English, sleep. |
| **Saturday evening allowance: 2 episodes, chosen in advance** | Decide *which* two before you start. Watch on a TV or laptop at a table — never in bed, never on the phone. |
| **Sunday: nothing on screen** | Sunday evening is for preparing the week and sleeping early. |
| **Never start a new series on a weekday** | New series are the binge trigger. |
| **Delete social media apps from the phone** | Use LinkedIn on the laptop only, for the job hunt. Everything else, check on desktop once a day if at all. |
| **Phone sleeps outside the bedroom** | Buy a ₨500 alarm clock. This single rule fixes both your mornings and your nights. |
| **Screen-time limit: 60 minutes non-work per day** | Use Digital Wellbeing on Android or Screen Time on iPhone. Put the phone in **grayscale**. |
| **Block adult content** | A DNS filter or a blocker on phone and laptop. It serves your health goals, your deen and your self-control at once. |
| **Relapse rule** | If you binge, do not punish yourself and do not "make up" for it. Write one line in LOG.md — what triggered it — and follow tomorrow's timetable exactly. |

**What fills the space.** Removing a habit without replacing it fails. The replacements are already in this timetable: the gym, the park, the Quran, family, the book, and the Saturday money-and-business hour.

## Composure, self-control and presence

Charisma is not a personality you are born with. It is mostly **calm, attention and reliability**, and every one of those can be practised.

**Daily habits**

1. **Keep small promises.** Wake for Fajr when you said you would. Self-respect is built from promises to yourself that you kept — and people can feel it.
2. **Pause before you answer.** Two seconds. It reads as confidence and prevents most of what you later regret.
3. **Speak slower and a little lower.** Nervous speech is fast speech. The Communication track in this book trains exactly this.
4. **Eye contact and posture.** Shoulders back, chin level. The gym gives you the body for it; you still have to use it.
5. **Listen more than you talk.** Ask one more question than you normally would. Remember names, and use them.
6. **Control anger.** *"The strong man is not the one who wrestles others; the strong man is the one who controls himself when angry."* (Bukhari) When angry: stay silent, sit down, make wudu.
7. **Dress clean and simple.** Ironed clothes, trimmed hair and beard, clean shoes. It costs little and changes how people treat you.

**The 5-minute muhasaba (every night, 22:00)** — three lines in a notebook:

- What did I do well today?
- Where did I lose control — of my time, my tongue, my eyes, my temper?
- What one thing will I do better tomorrow?

**Every month:** one deliberately uncomfortable social act — speak up in a meeting, attend a tech meetup, introduce yourself to someone senior, give a short talk. Composure is trained, like a squat.
